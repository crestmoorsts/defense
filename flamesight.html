Operational intelligence for autonomous systems

FlameSight is Crestmoor Defense's developing operational-intelligence layer, designed to transform fragmented sensor, autonomous-platform, mission, geospatial, logistics and human-generated data into a unified operational picture.

The FlameSight intelligence cycle

  1. SenseSources report what they observe.
  2. FuseEvidence is combined with its origin retained.
  3. UnderstandRelationships, anomalies and priorities come into view.
  4. RecommendA proposed course of action, with its rationale.
  5. AuthorizeA person decides.
  6. CoordinateCompatible systems act within the authorized scope.

Capabilities

What FlameSight is being designed to do.

  • Unified operational picture.One view across sensors, platforms and mission information.
  • Multi-source data fusion.Source identity and provenance stay attached to every piece of information.
  • Machine-assisted operational intelligence.Support for finding relationships, anomalies, priorities and mission-relevant information.
  • Human-governed autonomy.Explicit authorization boundaries between a recommendation and an action.
  • Edge-oriented design.Built for disconnected, degraded, intermittent or bandwidth-constrained environments.
  • Platform coordination.Coordination across compatible autonomous and semi-autonomous systems.

Trust architecture

Intelligence without provenance is assertion.

FlameSight is being designed around traceable operational reasoning, so information keeps its origin, confidence, time, relationships and authorization state as it moves through the system.

Observed → Inferred → Predicted → Recommended → Authorized → Executed

Each state stays distinct. A recommendation is never presented as an order.

Position

Crestmoor is not developing autonomous platforms in isolation. Crestmoor is developing the intelligence architecture that allows systems, sensors, operators and mission information to operate as one coordinated environment.

The machine should never be the commander. But command should never be blind.

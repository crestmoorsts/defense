Approach

How we build and how we describe it.

  • Human authority by design.Interlocks and approval points sit outside the AI decision loop, and red-team testing comes before any deployment.
  • Open architecture direction.We design toward DoD open-architecture standards such as MOSA and SOSA, and we do not claim conformance we have not demonstrated.
  • Supply chain awareness.We select components with NDAA and Blue UAS compliance in mind and review every dependency's license.
  • Plain maturity labels.We say whether a capability is a concept, a prototype or fielded, and we do not blur the three.

Leadership

The people behind the work.

Eugene Thomas

Founder and Chair

Eugene founded Crestmoor in 2025 and leads the company's technical direction and strategy. A U.S. Navy veteran who served as a Hospital Corpsman on operational deployments, he later worked as a Security Protective Officer with the Central Intelligence Agency.

He brings a decade of hands-on experience in electrical systems, industrial mechanics and maintenance, HVAC and refrigeration, and carpentry, with coursework in computer systems technology. That background shapes his approach to electromechanical integration, fault diagnosis, serviceability and the physical demands of robotic hardware.

John Love

Concept Developer, Robotics and Mechanical Systems

John is an independent technology innovator and hands-on mechanical professional with a lifelong interest in robotics, automation and emerging technology. He develops original robotics and mechanical-systems concepts and organizes them into product plans for discussion with technical, manufacturing and business partners.

His practical background includes manufacturing, generator systems, plumbing, mechanical troubleshooting, quality control and team supervision.
